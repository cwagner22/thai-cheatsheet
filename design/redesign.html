Thai Cheat Sheet — three directions for the whole app

What stays in all three: tone colours (red low · blue mid · purple falling · green high · pink rising — they carry meaning on every tab), the Serif/Sans Thai font switch, IPA in a monospace face, the dark spectrogram panels. What changes: the frame around the content — type, colour, how you move between the eight tabs, and how dense it is.

A — Notebook

Warm paper, a serif for headings (Fraunces), thin rules instead of boxes, tabs as a plain text row with an underline. Reads like a well-set reference book; quiet enough that the coloured Thai letters and the spectrograms are the only strong colour on the page. Best for the long reading tabs (Consonants, Reading, IPA).

B — Console

A dark top bar holds the title, a segmented tab control and the font switch; the page below is cool grey with white cards and uppercase section labels. Tighter, more tool-like — fits the Speaking and Typing tabs, where you press buttons and read numbers. Keyboard hints become part of the language.

C — Board

A left sidebar for the eight tabs (icons + names, always visible on a laptop; a bottom bar on a phone), a wider content column, soft floating cards, big Thai type. The most app-like of the three and the one that scales best as tabs are added; the font switch lives at the foot of the sidebar instead of floating over the page.
