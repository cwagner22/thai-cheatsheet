Sentence picker — three ways, one component each

Each replaces both the dropdown and the list at the bottom. All three keep ← → and show which sentences are yours; the pencil / “+” is where custom sentences get added and edited (saved in the browser).

A — side rail

A scrollable column to the right of the panels on wide screens: every sentence visible, grouped, one click to switch, the current one marked. Under about 1100 px the rail drops below the panels as the same list. The “+ Add a sentence” slot sits at the foot of the rail, and each of your own sentences gets a small pencil on hover.

B — chip strip

One horizontal, scrollable row of Thai chips right above the sentence, grouped by thin dividers with the group name turned on its side. Fast with a trackpad, works the same on every width, costs one row of height. A dashed “+” chip at the end adds a sentence; long-press or the pencil edits yours.

C — search box

A single field above the sentence. Click it (or press /) and the whole list drops down, filtered as you type — Thai or English — with ↑ ↓ Enter. Scales to hundreds of custom sentences without growing; the cost is one extra keystroke to see the list at all. Adding a sentence is the last row of the results.
